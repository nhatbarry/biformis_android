## Download provenance

`Tools/import_story_sfx.py` recreates the selection from three small publisher
archives cached under ignored `.utmp/story-sfx/`. It verifies SHA-256 hashes and
ZIP member CRCs, reads each pack's CC0 licence, validates Ogg/Vorbis framing and
duration, and imports only the listed effects and licences. It preserves existing
Unity GUIDs and refuses to overwrite changed media. Total added audio: 203,032
bytes. No full sound pack, preview or internet shortcut is included in Assets.

## Playback and dialogue

`Assets/Resources/Story SFX.asset` contains the 37 scene/boss cues and their volumes.
`Biformis > Audio > Configure Story SFX` rebuilds that catalog and compiles both Ink
languages. The scripts use `#sfx:name[:delaySeconds[:gain]]` beside the relevant
animation; repeated door knocks, struggle input, walking and boss attack frames
trigger their own cues. Audio sources belong to their scene actors and use the
existing SoundFX mixer, so the SFX setting controls them. Audio variation has a
separate RNG from gameplay. Skipping a cutscene cancels its pending cues.

Dialogue uses ten original, short mono PCM speech blips generated by
`Tools/generate_dialogue_sfx.py`: A bright, B lower, Mom warm, Doctor dry and
Villain raspy. Teen uses A's voice at a higher pitch; TeenB and A&B use B's voice.
Two variations per base voice play around every two letters at volume 0.28.
Spaces, punctuation, Narrator and System stay quiet. Voices stop on skip/disable.
These files replace the missing audio GUIDs in the old story profiles.

The final congrats montage retains its existing voices and applause, with new
typing blips muted while its timeline controls the dialogue. The two normal
A/B lines in the hospital epilogue resume dialogue blips. Current punctuation-only
placeholders in BeforeBoss and Ending remain silent until actual words are added.

The new external effects occupy 203 KB and original speech WAVs around 37 KB.
Volumes are intentionally modest; audible balance on the target phone can still
be adjusted in the catalog without changing the story timing.
